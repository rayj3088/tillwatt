    Where these numbers come from
    • Solar: US non-residential systems up to 100 kW installed in 2024 cost a median $3.20/W (middle 60%: $2.50–4.30) — LBNL, 2025 data update. Upkeep $20/kW-yr — NREL ATB 2024; inverter replaced about every 10 years at $0.19/W — NREL O&M cost model; panels fade about 0.5% a year — Jordan & Kurtz (NREL)
    • Batteries (LFP): about $314–653/kWh installed for commercial storage — Lazard LCOE+ 2025; 85% round trip, 2.5%/yr upkeep, 15-year life — NREL ATB 2024; 10-year calendar life — PNNL 2022
    • Diesel it replaces: US diesel averaged $3.76/gal in 2024 — EIA; a 30 kW generator burns 1.0–1.4 gal/h at 25–50% load — Generac spec sheet; small remote systems run $0.50–1+/kWh — DOE
    • Small wind: $7,370/kW installed, 13% average capacity factor — PNNL 2024; O&M $39/kW-yr — NREL ATB 2024. Micro-hydro: $3,400–10,000/kW, 40–60% capacity factor — IRENA-ETSAP
    • Hours for every job (prep, planting, weeding, watering, crop care, harvest, packing) — UC Cooperative Extension organic vegetable cost study. Wage $18.58/hr — USDA NASS, plus 43% payroll costs — UC 2024 cost study
    • Machines: FarmDroid FD20 €75,000–95,000 — Future Farming; Naïo Oz about €30,000 — Future Farming; farm-ng utility robot $12,990 — farm-ng; drip about $4,980/acre under 5 acres — NRCS 2025; washer $8,000 — UVM, packing line $8,085 — UMN
    • Invention briefs: harvest-robot benchmarks from Bac et al. 2014 and Kootstra et al. 2021. Prices for machines nobody sells are estimates, with wider error bars the less ready the idea is.
    • The 7-year target is the longest usual term of a USDA farm operating loan ("typically range from one to seven years") — FSA
    • 20-year value uses the USDA farm-ownership loan rate, 5.75% — FSA
    • Still estimates: how much of each job a machine takes over, machine upkeep and tending time, crop base power, lost harvest per dark hour, fertilizer and water hauling costs. The Research tab lists every number with its range.
